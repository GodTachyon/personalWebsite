## Logo insipration
Since this is the first blog post. Maybe, I go over why I chose "Sunraku" as the logo for my website. 
Its nothing that special. I just really, really, realllyyyy love his character and design. It just sparks joy the minute I look at him. For those unfamiliar [Shangri-La Frontier] is a novel, manga and anime series. Its about series about a protagonist who is a die hard addict of _Kusoge_ (bad games? is the literal translation). These are the type of games which tend to infuriate your average person when the game design, philosophy, attributes etc are setup in an "unconventional" way to make you hate your life. People who enjoy beating this experience are termed _Kusoge Gamers_. The protaganists character in the main game "SLF" (wonder how they settled on this abbreviation 👀).

Its quite a nice story with good grasp on "gamery" things lets say. I thoroughly enjoy reading and admiring the artists interpretation of the characters. Sunraku is a guy who is always happy, loves a good challenge, has keen insigts and very open to interaction and people. Hence, my use of Sunraku as the logo.
